# PVArr 0.16.0

Everything in [0.15.1](v0.15.1.md), plus:

- **Steps down only when your connection is the problem.** PVArr keeps the best quality. If
  segments go missing because they time out (four such losses within five minutes), it switches to a
  lower quality of the same stream, or to a lighter backup that is a different feed. It goes back up
  after 20 quiet minutes, stays down if the trouble comes straight back, and steps down at most three
  times per recording. A source that answers "not found" or a server error never triggers it: that
  is the source, not your connection. Most event pages offer a single quality, so this often has
  nothing lighter to switch to; you get one notification saying so.
- **Break cards found by repetition.** With `PVARR_COMSKIP=1`, the break-card pass now marks a
  stretch as a break when its pictures recur from another break at least five minutes away, for 45
  seconds or more. Sources show the same card in every break; live play never repeats a picture. The
  old "quiet audio and no score overlay" rule found nothing on recordings stitched from several
  sources; the new one found every card checked on two recordings. A card shown only once is missed,
  and anything else shown twice (a long black or "stream offline" slate) can be marked.

**Upgrading:** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, ideally while nothing is recording.
No settings change. The image is about 70 MB larger (numpy, for the card pass). The card pass now
takes 3 to 10 minutes of one niced core per game, after the recording is already in your library.
